01 — Services In-Depth

OUR DETAILED OFFERINGS

Sanitaryware Solutions

Premium bathroom fixtures, faucets, and vanity units curated for style and strength.

  • Luxury Faucets & Closets: Partnered with Cera, Somany and Hindware for advanced water-closets and elegant mixers.
  • Vanity & Bath Enclosures: Custom design vanity basins, glass cubicles, and anti-fog mirror arrays.
  • Advanced Plumbing Conduits: Prince and Ashirvad heavy-duty water pipelines to prevent leakages entirely.

Electrical & Lighting

Safe wiring setups, heavy-duty switchgears, and bespoke decorative illumination layouts.

  • Certified Power Wiring: Sourcing fire-retardant copper cables from top-tier brands.
  • Switchgear & Panels: Complete industrial and domestic safety breakers, panel boards, and sockets.
  • Bespoke LED Layouts: Recessed spotlights, luxury chandeliers, and profile strip lights for modern homes.

Premium Tiles Solutions

Wide catalog of vitrified, double-charge, porcelain, and mosaic tiling finishes.

  • Vitrified Flooring Tiles: Large-format luxury tiles replicating marble textures with high scratch resistance.
  • Cladding Wall Tiles: Textured cladding wall panels, kitchen backsplash mosaics, and bathroom decors.
  • Heavy Traffic & Outdoors: Slip-resistant rustic parking tiles and weather-proof outdoor paving blocks.

Plumbing Engineering & Advisory

Tailored estimations, material selections guidance, and synchronized delivery logistics.

  • Free Material Estimation: Submit blueprints for an exact, optimized bill-of-materials calculation.
  • Expert Design Match: Advisory sessions to match sanitaryware colors with tiling gradients.
  • Doorstep Logistics Delivery: Fleet logistics guaranteeing safe, break-free arrival directly at site.
